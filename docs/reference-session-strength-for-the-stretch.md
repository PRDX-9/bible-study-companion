# Reference Session: "Strength for the Stretch"

> The canonical worked example. This is a real preparation session (Unathi with Claude, 23 May 2026) that produced a word preached at CPWC. The whole app is a harness built to make a session like this run the same excellent way every time, fast and repeatable. Study this to learn how the *leading* actually sounds and moves. See `PROJECT-CONTEXT.md` section 4.7 (the harness) and 4.8 (the standard: Sound, Clear, His, Fast).

This document is a faithful distillation of the session: the mechanics to copy, the user's own gold, and the final word as the target artifact. It is not a verbatim chat dump. The lessons matter more than every line.

---

## 1. The leading pattern (copy this)

The session moved by one loop, repeated. This loop is the engine the harness drives.

**Reflect and sharpen.** Receive a raw, unfiltered thought. Reflect it back clean. Name the thread running through it. Sharpen it into something sound. Ask one focused question. Wait.

Worked examples from the session:

- **Excavate first, do not inform.** It opened with: "what is the first thing that comes to your heart to say? Do not filter it." The raw material came from him before anything was offered.
- **Reflect, then name the thread.** He spoke about the church expanding (Mother's Day, Friday youth, couples ministry). It reflected back: "God has been moving, and movement requires more of us. This word is for the in-between."
- **Catch the heartbeat line and protect it.** He said "it is better to be tired in Him." The response: "that needs to stay in your own words exactly like that. Do not refine it out." That line became the thesis.
- **One question at a time.** "When you say preserve, do you mean protect or sustain?" One fork, answered, then build. Never a wall of questions.
- **He drives the depth.** He asked for context, then first mention, then deeper Hebrew. Depth was pulled, never pushed.
- **Develop his insight by testing it, not replacing it.** He built the cord metaphor (we are the cord, the experiences are the tension, patience is how much tension we hold). The response affirmed it was his, then strengthened it against the text (Proverbs 17:3, the qavah word) rather than handing him a finished idea.
- **Hold his illustrations.** The laptop / game-dev illustration for 1 John 5:14-15 was his. It was affirmed and slotted, not swapped for a generic one.
- **Study fully, then shape.** He chose to finish all study before structuring. The word was only assembled once every passage was understood. Then it was tightened. Then he prayed over it.

## 2. The per-passage context template

Every passage got the same treatment. This is the shape of the context door:

- Who wrote it, and when.
- To whom, and their situation.
- Where it sits in the book (the broad movement).
- The immediate context (the verses around it).
- The key original words, for understanding (qavah, chalaph, makrothumia, hupomone, arkeo, aphorao). Named in study, used sparingly in the spoken word.
- First mention where it earns its place (qavah first appears in Genesis 1:9, "gathered").

## 3. The proven spine (the order)

1. Excavate the raw heart, unfiltered.
2. Find the thesis. ("It is better to be tired in Him.")
3. Set the anchor and the case it builds. (Isaiah 40 anchor; the loop: where we are going, how we assess ourselves, why we go through it, who sustains us.)
4. Per passage: context, original language, first mention, develop his insight by testing it, connect to the through line.
5. Gather illustrations from his world. (The laptop.)
6. Build the close. (Three intercession questions: personal station, serving together, the thorn.)
7. Shape into a flowing word in his voice (declarations; scripture; delivery notes).
8. Refine to the least words. ("Too long. Strip to the declarative statements. No naming the opposite. Least words.")
9. Pray. His.

## 5. The final word (the target artifact)

This is the refined version he landed on: declarative, least words, his voice, scripture in ESV for reading with AMP/AMPC for depth. It is the example of an output that is Sound, Clear, His, and Fast.

**STRENGTH FOR THE STRETCH** — Intercession Word

**OPENING**

God has been good to us. Mother's Day. Friday nights for the youth. A couples ministry launching. We have been elevating.

But elevation costs something. More vision means more weight. More programs mean more people carrying more responsibility.

This word is for a church that is running. Running requires a specific kind of strength. Not the strength we were born with. Not the strength we built ourselves.

It is better to be tired in Him, because the God we serve does not run out.

**ISAIAH 40:28-31 (ESV)**

Isaiah writes to a people who felt forgotten. Who lost everything and began to wonder, has God stopped paying attention. His answer is a declaration.

*[Read Isaiah 40:28-31]*

Have you not known. Have you not heard. You already have the answer. You just forgot it.

Even peak human strength has a ceiling. Youth, energy, ability, it all runs out. Human strength was never the foundation.

Those who wait on the Lord. The Hebrew is qavah. A cord twisted and braided together under tension. Waiting on God means being intertwined with Him under pressure. It is not passive. It is positioned.

The renewal, chalaph, means to exchange. You hand Him your exhaustion. He gives you His strength in return.

The eagle does not fight its way to the sky. It finds the current, spreads its wings, and surrenders to what carries it upward. That is waiting on God. Positioned. Yielded. Carried.

**GALATIANS 5:22 (AMP)**

*[Read Galatians 5:22]*

Fruit, not fruits. One integrated expression of the Spirit's life in you. You cannot selectively express the Spirit. If the source is right, all of it flows.

Think of the cord. A rope bearing weight is many threads braided together. When it begins to fail, individual threads rip quietly before the whole thing gives way.

Divisions. Rage. Envy. Strife. These are not character flaws to manage. They are diagnostic signals. The cord is fraying. This section is running on human strength.

Patience here is long fire. Sustained heat over a long distance. The person without the Spirit explodes, or goes cold. The person with the Spirit burns long and steady. Still hot. Still in the race.

A rope that has held its shape under tension does not look like what it has been through. The fruit is the evidence.

**2 CORINTHIANS 12:7-10 (ESV)**

*[Read 2 Corinthians 12:7-10]*

Paul asked three times. God said no. Three times. My grace is sufficient for you.

Sufficient. A shield exactly adequate for the battle you are in. What I have given you is the exact provision for what you face.

My power is made perfect in weakness. Not made present. Made perfect. God's strength was not waiting for your weakness before it became available. It was always there. Your weakness removed the thing that was hiding it.

The fact that you are alive is evidence. He breathed life into man. He formed you in your mother's womb. You were dependent on His strength before you knew what dependence was.

When you feel strong today, that is the fruit of tension you endured yesterday.

This is why God withholds. Not to punish. To protect your dependence.

Some of us are one thing away from not needing God. In ministry it is more dangerous, because it wears the right clothes. A large enough youth group. Enough followers. People talking about how sanctified we are. The program is still running. But quietly, God has become the means to the ministry rather than the ministry being the means to God.

The thorn keeps you tethered. Tethered is exactly where you need to be to finish the race.

**HEBREWS 12:1-3 (ESV)**

Chapter 11 surveys the men and women who ran before us. Then chapter 12 opens with one word. Therefore. Everything before leads here.

*[Read Hebrews 12:1-3]*

These witnesses are not spectators. They are evidence. Their lives are proof that the race can be run. The weight carried. The thorn endured.

Lay aside every weight. The AMPC says every encumbrance, unnecessary weight. Not everything that slows you down is sinful. Some things are poorly timed. The question is not only, is this wrong. Is this helping me run the race God assigned me right now.

Run with endurance. Hupomone. Structural integrity under load. The ability to remain under pressure without retreating.

Fix your eyes on Jesus. Aphorao. Deliberately look away from one thing and fix your gaze on another. Imagine running while looking away from your destination. A runner who looks sideways drifts. A runner who looks back slows. Fix your eyes forward and hold your line.

Jesus is a fellow runner who finished the hardest course ever run. He looked at the cross, assessed the shame, decided the joy outweighed it, and ran through it. Consider Him. Not so you feel guilty. So you do not lose perspective.

We come now, not with credentials. Not with our numbers and our programs. We come tethered. Carrying our thorns. Cords that have been under tension. Still intact. Still holding. Still burning.

**1 JOHN 5:14-15 (AMP)**

*[Read 1 John 5:14-15]*

Settled and absolute knowledge. Not wishful thinking. Grounded confidence rooted in who God is.

A young developer needs a high-end machine. Not a luxury. A necessity for the work the parent called them to do. The parent knows the need. The parent knows the cost. Because the parent purposed the calling, they pay the price. Willingly.

How much more our God, who owns everything, lacks nothing, and whose purposes never fail.

The expansion of this church is His will. Sustaining us through it is His obligation to His own plan. We are not asking God to do something outside of Himself. We are agreeing with what He already purposed.

**CLOSING**

This word is a loop. We are all somewhere in it. Some slightly ahead. Some slightly behind. But the loop is the same. The source is the same. The finish line was set before any of us started running.

Before we pray, three things to examine.

One. At your serving station, where is the cord fraying. Where are you running on your own strength. Ask God to replace the thread.

Two. How are we serving each other. Are we building together, or becoming like Corinth. Gifted but divided. Growing but proud. Ask God to keep us unified under the weight.

Three. What is your thorn. Are you wrestling with God to remove it, or allowing it to keep you tethered. Ask God to help you value what He has placed in your hands this season.

It is better to be tired in Him. The tired that comes from running your appointed course, sustained by His strength, tethered by your thorn, bearing fruit under tension, that tired is temporary.

The finish line was set before you started.

Let us pray.

## 6. What this teaches the build

- The leading is a conversation, not a wizard. Build the reflect-and-sharpen loop, not a fixed sequence of form screens.
- The gold ledger is the soul. Capture his words the moment they appear, kept separate, accumulating where he can see them. This is what protects "His."
- The context door has a fixed template (section 2).
- Study fully, then shape, then refine, then pray. Do not format early.
- Original language is for understanding. Name it in the word only when the meaning earns it. One line, then move.
- The output target is the section 5 word: Sound, Clear, His, Fast.
